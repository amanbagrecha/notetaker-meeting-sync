# Meeting Notebook

A fused-render app that keeps one notebook page per day: the meetings you had (summary, what was discussed, decisions) and the to-dos you personally own from them. A background Claude task syncs meetings from Wispr Flow and Google Calendar; you can tick, add, edit or move to-dos, keep a private notes thread inside any to-do (updates, drafts, links and pasted or dropped images — click the to-do to open it), chat with a meeting's transcript, and turn hand-picked to-dos into tickets in your team's Notion ticket database. Claude reads that database, writes a clear title from the to-do and its notes, assigns whoever is doing the work and copies the notes and images into the ticket. The first time, it finds the database for you, and the app remembers it.

Needs fused-render with Claude tasks enabled, plus the claude.ai Wispr Flow, Google Calendar and (for tickets) Notion connectors. All notebook data stays local in `.fused/data/`. Note images are stored in `.fused/data/attachments/`, so like the rest of the notebook they stay on this Mac and are not part of app exports. The exception is a to-do you send to Notion: its notes and images go into the ticket.
